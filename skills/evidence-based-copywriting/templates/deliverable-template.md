# Self-review checklist

<!-- Mark honestly. A checklist item marked [x] that a reviewer can falsify
     does more damage than the defect it hides. -->

- [ ] Every factual claim has a direct web source or exact internal document citation.
- [ ] Nothing contradicts the project's source documents.
- [ ] The copy contains zero invented proof (customers, testimonials, logos, case studies, certifications, partnerships, metrics, results).
- [ ] External market evidence is not presented as proof of this product's performance.
- [ ] Every claim about the product is supported by the project's own documents.
- [ ] Every external source is credible, current, dated, and linked directly (no homepages where deeper pages exist).
- [ ] Verified facts, internal claims, projections, recommendations, and judgments are labeled and distinguishable.
- [ ] Superlatives appear only where the cited source itself uses them.
- [ ] All quotes are verbatim; all paraphrases preserve unit, population, and date.
- [ ] Projections and design targets are explicitly labeled, everywhere they appear.
- [ ] Each hero passes the five-second test (what, for whom, action).
- [ ] Every section survives: "Would a skeptical [buyer persona] believe this from this company at its actual stage?"
- [ ] The copy reads aloud cleanly; no sentence would embarrass a founder on a sales call.
- [ ] `check_copy.py` passes (banned lexicon + embedded limits) and the citation worksheet was reviewed.
